<br>There's something I wish someone had told me when I was starting out.<br><br>

The perfect moment to launch your digital business is not coming.<br><br>

It's never going to feel like the perfect time. You'll never feel completely ready. There will always be another course to take, something else to learn, or another reason to wait.<br><br>

And while you wait, someone else is launching.<br><br>

## What waiting actually costs you.<br><br>

Every month you don't launch a digital product is a month you don't have that income.<br><br>

If your product earns $300/month, waiting six months costs you $1,800. Waiting a year costs you $3,600.<br><br>

It's not just about the money. It's about your time, and that's something you can't get back.<br><br>

## The myth of "being ready."<br><br>

Here's the truth about being ready: you only get ready by taking action, not by waiting until you feel ready.<br><br>

The first time I published an e-book, I didn't feel ready. The first time I launched a class, I didn't feel ready.<br><br>

The first time I built my Academy Digital community, I didn't feel ready.<br><br>

I launched anyway. Each time, I learned more than I ever did from any course.<br><br>

## The real reason people wait<br><br>

It's not a lack of knowledge.<br><br>

It's fear.<br><br>

Fear of judgment. Fear of failure. Fear of being seen. Fear of launching something imperfect.<br><br>

I hear you. I felt all of that. And I want to offer you this perspective:<br><br>

A real, imperfect product can help someone. A perfect idea that never leaves your head doesn't help anyone.<br><br>

## The one question that changed everything for me<br><br>

When I was procrastinating on my first launch, someone asked me:<br><br>

"What's the worst realistic thing that could happen if you publish this today?"<br><br>

I thought about it honestly. The answer was: a few people might not like it.<br><br>

And I asked myself: can I survive that?<br><br>

Yes.<br><br>

So I published.<br><br>

## Your action for today<br><br>

Not this week. Not when you finish the course. Not when you feel ready.<br><br>

Today.<br><br>

Open a Google Doc. Write the title of the product you've been thinking about. Write the first sentence.<br><br>

That's it. That's your start. Keep going and keep writing your product now.<br><br>

The moment is now. It always was.<br><br>
